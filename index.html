      💸 Bills & Sinking Funds
      Each paycheck automatically sets aside a fraction of each bill so you're never surprised. The full bill is paid from the reserve when due.
      🎯 Savings Targets
      A one-off thing you're saving for by a certain date. Each paycheck puts aside what's left divided by the paychecks left, so it lands on time.
